Sections

A <neuwo-nav-section> takes its name in label and holds <neuwo-nav-item> entries and further sections, to any depth.

  • A section is marked and opens when it holds the current page, or when the page lies below one of its entries, as a page under Guides that the nav does not list would.
  • Only the reader closes a section. When the current page moves, as a client-side router moves it, the sections that were open stay open, and the new page's sections open as well.
  • open starts a section open. id names it for remembering its state; without one, its label does.

The entry for this page carries a <neuwo-badge> in its label, since an entry's label is any inline markup.

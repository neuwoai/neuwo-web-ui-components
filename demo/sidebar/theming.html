Theming

The column reads its width from --neuwo-sidebar-width, 17rem when it is not set. Set it on the element or an ancestor; the library never declares it, so nothing it ships can override yours.

The breakpoint is fixed at 48rem, since a custom property cannot drive a media query. Below it the sidebar is a bar on --neuwo-glass-bg with --neuwo-glass-blur behind it, stacked on --neuwo-z-header.

Every other part is a prefixed class, .neuwo-sidebar and its elements, which a page restyles with a class rule. The entries are .neuwo-nav-link with a tighter gutter, so they move with any change to the navigation look, and so are the sections' names, a size smaller once they are nested.

A section's chevron is drawn in --neuwo-color-text-muted, lifts to --neuwo-color-text while its row is hovered, and turns over --neuwo-transition-fast, without moving at all when the reader asks for reduced motion.

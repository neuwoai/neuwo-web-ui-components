Getting Started

This page and the three beside it share one hand-written <neuwo-sidebar>. None of them sets current, so the element takes the page's path from the address bar and marks the entry that names it: the underline and aria-current="page" follow you from page to page.

Things to Try

  • Follow the entries. Each page marks its own entry, and the column stays where it is as the page scrolls.
  • Narrow the window below 48rem. The column becomes a glass bar pinned to the top, with the menu button at its end.
  • Open the menu and follow an entry. The menu closes, and every page starts with it closed.
  • Open the menu, then widen the window again. The column shows the nav whatever the menu was left as.
  • Press Tab. The brand, the entries and the footer link are ordinary links in reading order.

The Places

The wordmark is in the brand slot, centred at the top of the column and at the start of the bar. The All Components button is in the actions slot, which only the bar shows. The hint under the nav is in the after-nav slot, and Back to neuwo.ai is in the footer slot, ruled off at the bottom of the column.

The column is 17rem wide, sticky at the top and the full viewport tall, so a long nav scrolls inside it while the footer stays in view. The page around it is the site's own: a flex row, the content capped at 48rem and centred in the space that is left.

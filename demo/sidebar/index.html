Getting Started

This page and the six in its nav share one hand-written <neuwo-sidebar>. None of them sets current, so the element takes the page's path from the address bar, marks the entry that names it and opens the sections above it: the underline and aria-current="page" follow you from page to page. Each sets remember, so the sections you leave open follow you too.

Things to Try

  • Follow the entries. Each page marks its own entry and the sections above it, and the column stays where it is as the page scrolls.
  • Fold Guides from anywhere on its row, with a click, or with Enter or Space. Reference folds from the chevron beside its link.
  • Narrow the window below 48rem. The column becomes a glass bar pinned to the top, with the menu button at its end.
  • Open the menu and follow an entry. The menu closes, and every page starts with it closed.
  • Open the menu, then widen the window again. The column shows the nav whatever the menu was left as.
  • Press Tab. The brand, the entries, the sections' rows and the footer link come in reading order.

Remembered Sections

With remember, the sidebar keeps your place in the nav for this tab. Open In Depth, then follow Installation: In Depth is still open there, though Installation does not hold it. Make the window short enough that the nav scrolls, scroll it and follow an entry, and the next page's nav starts where you left it. Close Guides and it opens again on the next page, since open starts it open there: remembering only ever opens a section. A new tab starts with only the current page's sections open.

Move the Mark

A site with a client-side router sets current as the reader moves between pages, and the sidebar stays in place. These buttons set it the same way without leaving this page. Open Reference, then mark Theming: In Depth opens and takes the underline, and Reference stays open, since only the reader closes a section.

The Places

The wordmark is in the brand slot, centred at the top of the column and at the start of the bar. The All Components button is in the actions slot, which only the bar shows; at 320px it no longer fits beside the wordmark, so it takes a row of its own at the bar's end, clear of the menu button. The hint under the nav is in the after-nav slot, and Back to neuwo.ai is in the footer slot, ruled off at the bottom of the column.

The column is 17rem wide, sticky at the top and the full viewport tall, so a long nav scrolls inside it while the footer stays in view. The page around it is the site's own: a flex row, the content capped at 48rem and centred in the space that is left.

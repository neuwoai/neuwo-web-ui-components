Accessibility

The sidebar is an <aside>, a complementary landmark, holding a <nav> named by label. The brand and the footer sit outside the nav, so they are not announced as part of it.

The entries are links in a list, announced with its count, and the current page's link carries aria-current="page". There is no tree widget and no roving focus: Tab moves through the links as on any page.

The menu button below 48rem is the <summary> of a <details>, named by menu-label. The browser announces its expanded state, and on a server-rendered page it opens and closes the menu before the library's script has run, and without it.

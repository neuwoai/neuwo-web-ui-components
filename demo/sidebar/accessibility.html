Accessibility

The sidebar is an <aside>, a complementary landmark, holding a <nav> named by label. The brand and the footer sit outside the nav, so they are not announced as part of it.

The entries are links in a list, announced with its count, and the current page's link carries aria-current="page". There is no tree widget and no roving focus: Tab moves through the links as on any page, with each section's row between its neighbours.

A section is a <details> whose <summary> is its row, so the browser announces it as expanded or collapsed, and Enter and Space fold it. A section with an href has its link first, then a separate toggle named from its label, such as Show Reference pages. Only the reader closes a section: when the current page moves, the open sections stay open.

The menu button below 48rem is the <summary> of a <details>, named by menu-label. The browser announces its expanded state, and on a server-rendered page it opens and closes the menu before the library's script has run, and without it.

Guides

Guides is a section that only folds. Its name fills the row, and a click anywhere on the row, or Enter or Space on it, folds or unfolds it. Its own page, this one, is the Introduction entry, first inside it.

That is the form to prefer. A name that both links and folds is two targets in one row, and on a phone a tap meant to look inside the section takes the reader away instead. Reference, further down the nav, shows the other form.

Guides carries open, so it starts open on every page. In Depth does not: it opens on Theming and Accessibility, the pages it holds, and takes the underline there along with Guides.
